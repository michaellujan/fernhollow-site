Fernhollow

A cozy merge game about an old plant nursery, for iPhone and iPad.

Privacy policySupport

How it plays

Your great-aunt Odile left you her nursery, shut for eleven years and lost under brambles. Tap the seed box, merge seedlings, pots and tools on the potting bench, and fill the villagers' orders. Their thanks pay for restoring the nursery task by task, with Wren, the girl from over the fence, appointing herself your assistant. Seven areas, and in each one another of Odile's letters, telling at last why she locked the gate.
